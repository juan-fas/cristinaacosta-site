Buyers who move here from Ontario or British Columbia are braced for a land transfer tax. Alberta does not have one. What Alberta has instead is a set of smaller costs that still add up to real money on closing day, and you pay them on top of your down payment.

## Land title registration fees

When the property transfers to you, Alberta Land Titles charges a registration fee based on the value of the property. When your mortgage is registered against it, a second fee is charged based on the mortgage amount. Both are a base charge plus a small amount per $5,000 of value. On a $550,000 home with a $495,000 mortgage, the two together come to a few hundred dollars, not the several thousand a transfer tax would cost. Our [closing costs calculator](/calculators/closing-costs) shows the exact figure.

## Legal fees

A lawyer handles the transfer, the mortgage registration and the money on closing day. Fees and disbursements for a straightforward purchase usually land between $1,200 and $2,000 in Calgary. Ask for a quote that includes disbursements.

## Title insurance

Most lenders require it. A one-time premium, typically a few hundred dollars, that protects the lender and, if you choose owner coverage, you, against title defects and fraud.

## Appraisal

Some lenders order an appraisal to confirm the value, especially on refinances and unusual properties. Budget $300 to $500. Some lenders cover it.

## Home inspection

Not required by anyone, and worth every dollar on a resale home. $400 to $600 for a typical house.

## Adjustments

If the seller has already paid property tax or condo fees for the period after your closing date, you reimburse them for your share on closing day. A tax bill paid in June for the whole year, on a September closing, means you owe the seller roughly a third of it.

## Interest adjustment

If you close mid-month, the lender collects the interest from your closing date to the first regular payment date up front.

## Home insurance

The lender needs proof that the home is insured from the closing date. The first year's premium is often paid at or before closing.

## GST on new homes

Resale homes carry no GST. New builds do: 5% of the price, though rebates exist and change over time. Confirm with the builder how the price is stated and whether the rebate is assigned to them.

## How much to have ready

For a resale home in Alberta, a working rule is about 1.5% of the price, plus the down payment. New builds are higher because of GST.
